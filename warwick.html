Warwick University and its contribution to modern technology

The £700 million campus expansion

Warwick University is carrying out a £700 million campus expansion, the largest investment in its history.

This expansion will focus on Social Sciences and STEM, helping to drive technology research and education.

The impact of WMG

WMG is a major academic department/research centre at Warwick.

It serves as one of the UK's High Value Manufacturing Catapult centres.

WMG offers apprenticeship programs, as well as direct research support for industrial partners.
