Mental Health Awareness

Mental health is an important aspect of overall well-being. It encompasses emotional, psychological, and social well-being, affecting how we think, feel, and act.

Here is an article on this topic: Mental Health

Everyday ways to support your wellbeing

Area A small step
Rest Make time for a regular sleep routine and breaks.
Connection Talk with someone you trust, even if it is just a quick check-in.
Daily routine Choose one manageable activity, such as a short walk or a meal.

Getting support is okay

If difficult feelings are lasting or affecting daily life, consider talking with someone you trust or a health professional. This page is general information, not a diagnosis.
